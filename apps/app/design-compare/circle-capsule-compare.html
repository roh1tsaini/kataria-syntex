Circle & capsule controls — Apple-style shell grammar

Rule 1 — one lone icon button is a circle

32px on desktop, 44px on touch. Hairline border, transparent at rest, muted fill on hover, scale 0.9 on press.

Rule 2 — two or more adjacent icon buttons join into one capsule

Every button inside stays a circle; the capsule is one muted pill holding them, with a top specular edge like lit glass. Vertical variant for the rail.

Rule 3 — tab bar: spring-sliding pill, circle when single

The selection pill glides between tabs on a damped spring — squetching as it travels, settle without bounce. Click fast: it retargets mid-flight from its current position and speed.

On the phone — floating frosted bar + sheet

The capsule floats over content on a frosted material; tapping a tab slides the pill and pops the icon. Tap Home again to pop the sheet up.

Sheet — springs up from the bar

Sidebar rail — vertical capsule on desktop

The collapsed desktop rail holds its icon actions in one vertical capsule, circles inside.

Performance: springs are transform-only and the loop stops on settle; blur is a single modest layer with solid fallbacks for prefers-reduced-transparency and no-backdrop-filter browsers.
