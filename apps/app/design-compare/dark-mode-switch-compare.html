The switch

Control animation

Six ways the button itself changes. The flip is a plain swap here — screen transitions are in the next gallery.

A

Crossfade & tilt

Each icon tilts 45° as it fades; the other tilts in.

  • Motion — crossfade with a 45° tilt per icon.
  • Timing — 200ms in · 140ms out · press 100ms scale .97.
  • Reduced motion — fade only, no tilt.
B

Sun ↔ moon morph

One shape changes: rays pull away, the crescent is carved out.

  • Motion — SVG mask morph: rays retract, crescent cuts in.
  • Timing — 380ms morph curve in · 300ms out.
  • Reduced motion — crossfade, no morph.
C

Slot roll

Vertical reel: sun rolls up out of frame as moon rolls up into it.

  • Motion — the disc becomes a reel; the old icon leaves upward, the new follows in.
  • Timing — 380ms morph curve in · 300ms out.
  • Reduced motion — straight swap.
D

Day/night pill

A track with a gliding knob; the waiting icon rests at the far end.

  • Motion — knob glides, the active icon rides it, the other waits muted at the far end.
  • Timing — 380ms morph curve · press 100ms scale .97.
  • Trade-off — 68×40px, not a circle; changes the icon-button grammar and needs header room.
E

Cross-rotate Apple

Control Center's move: both glyphs rotate through the swap — the old spins out, the new spins in.

  • Motion — 260ms ease-in-out rotate + 200ms crossfade.
  • Timing — press 100ms scale .97 · lags nothing mid-flip.
  • Reduced motion — fade only, no rotation.
F

Segmented picker Apple

Two halves, one thumb — the picker Apple uses in Settings. Tap a half; the thumb morphs over.

  • Motion — thumb slides 380ms morph · glyphs swap 200ms.
  • Trade-off — 88×40px (96×44px phone), shows both themes at once.

The flip

Screen transition

Ten ways the whole window changes theme. Every option starts at your tap point where it makes sense, and treats the disc/page as the actor — click a switch and watch the window, not the card. Identical code on web desktop, phone web, Electron and Android. Reduced motion swaps instantly; without View Transitions it is a plain swap.

T1

Circle from the tap point

The new theme grows as a disc from the button; the radius always covers the farthest corner.

  • Mechanism — disc expands from the tap point.
  • Motion — 380ms ease-out · the Telegram/Android classic.
T2

Soft circle

Same disc, but the leading edge is feathered — no hard boundary anywhere.

  • Mechanism — disc with a 64px soft edge.
  • Motion — 380ms ease-out · calmest of the two discs.
T3

Curtain

The new theme drops from the top edge and covers the full width — a window shade, not a disc.

  • Mechanism — full-width band sweeps top to bottom.
  • Motion — 380ms ease-out · no origin point needed.
T4

Diagonal sweep

A triangle sweeps from the top-left corner across the screen.

  • Mechanism — corner triangle opens across the viewport.
  • Motion — 380ms ease-out · one diagonal pass.
T5

Blinds

Eight horizontal bands snap open, top to bottom — mechanical, 50ms apart.

  • Mechanism — 8 steps, top band first.
  • Motion — 400ms total · steps(8) · the boldest option.
T6

Zoom bloom

The disc grows from the button while the whole layer settles from 94% scale — the new theme arrives with weight.

  • Mechanism — disc + a 0.94→1 scale settle.
  • Motion — 380ms ease-out · reads like the theme arrives, not cuts.
T7

Fade

Plain crossfade with an 8px blur on entry. The no-risk default.

  • Mechanism — opacity + blur crossfade.
  • Motion — 380ms ease-out · cheapest on weak devices.
T8

Wallpaper settle Apple

macOS dynamic desktop style — the window softly relaxes into the new theme with dual-layer depth settle.

  • Mechanism — dual-layer scale settle (1.02→1 / 1→0.98) + cross-dissolve.
  • Motion — 450ms smooth curve · calm, photographic depth.
T9

Radar sweep Apple

Clockwise conic sweep centered at the tap point — the Apple Watch circular gauge and face wipe.

  • Mechanism — 360° conic mask originating from the button.
  • Motion — 440ms ease-out · precise, radial wipe.
T10

App-open zoom Apple

iOS Springboard expansion — the new theme blooms outward directly from the switch's rounded perimeter.

  • Mechanism — rounded inset clip-path expanding from switch bounds.
  • Motion — 420ms spring curve · physically anchored to the switch.
