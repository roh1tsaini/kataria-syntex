Brand colour — four directions

Identical layout, identical type scale, identical components in every direction. Colour is the variable — each direction also carries a display/mono pairing that suits it, so judge the palette first and the faces second. Each one is shown on a real slice of the site — nav, hero, shade panel, stat band, yarn cards, the 140-shade strip, form controls, footer — in light and dark. Toggle either. Hover and focus states are live. Contrast ratios are computed from the tokens at runtime, not typed in by hand.

Why the current palette fails

  • Everything is one pale blue. Page, cards, panels, art, footer — no value contrast, so nothing reads as foreground.
  • The duotone destroys the product. All six yarn photos multiply into the same royal field. Cotton and polyester are indistinguishable.
  • The product is the colour. A full-spectrum shade card cannot sit inside a coloured chrome — the chrome has to recede.
  • Print. Each direction still resolves to one solid + one spot, so the visiting card and shade card stay printable.
