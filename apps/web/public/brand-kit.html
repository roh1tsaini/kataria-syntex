Kataria Syntex — brand & web kit

One page for every open decision: the text-only logo (KS + Kataria Syntex), a library of colour schemes, the visiting card, and the website rebuild directions. Pick a palette and a logo face in the bar above — everything below re-renders live. Light/dark switch checks the dark build. Nothing here is final; it is a picker.

Current lock (baseline) The live site runs #0A2540 navy, #1E3A8A royal, #7DD3FC sky, #B8D8F5 ice (owner-locked, see apps/web/design.md). Every other scheme here is a challenger.
How to read a palette card Ink carries dark surfaces and headings. Primary carries actions and product art. Accent is the single spot on dark — numbers, tags, card back. Print stays to one solid + one spot.
Logo rules Text only — no drawn symbol. The KS monogram is the short mark (favicon, card, avatar); the wordmark is the lockup next to it. Nothing is traced or illustrated.
What happens after you pick Chosen palette → globals.css tokens + design.md §2.1; chosen lockup → header, footer, card and favicon. Then the pages get rebuilt in the chosen direction. This page is deleted once decisions are locked.

02 · Colour

Colour schemes — 20 directions, five families

Every scheme is built from three values: ink (dark surfaces, headings), primary (actions, art field) and accent (the spot on dark). Surfaces, hairlines and muted text are derived from those three — swap them and the whole system re-tints. Ratios are computed live, CMYK is an approximation for the card proof.

03 · Print

Visiting card — six builds

Cards are 85 × 53.5 mm. Front carries the mark, name and role; back carries the contact ledger. All six read the selected palette and logo face, so change either and re-judge. Print rule: one solid + one spot — nothing else goes to press.

04 · Website

Website directions — 24 languages

The same offer in twenty-four design languages, organised by category. Each is a live miniature of the real page — nav, hero, actions, product data, shade strip — and every one re-tints with the palette you picked. Hover states are real; dark mode is real.

05 · Spec

Tokens & print ledger

The selected scheme as a token table: what each value does, its hex, and a screen→print CMYK approximation. Lock the exact recipe with a physical proof before any print run — on screen, hex is exact.
